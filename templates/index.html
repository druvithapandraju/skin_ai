🩺 Important Skin Disease Awareness

1. Early Detection Saves Lives

Skin cancer, especially melanoma, is highly treatable when detected early.

2. ABCDE Rule

Asymmetry, Border, Color, Diameter, and Evolving help identify dangerous moles.

3. UV Radiation Risk

Excess sun exposure is the leading cause of most skin cancers.

4. Irregular Borders

Moles with uneven or jagged edges can be a warning sign.

5. Color Variation

Multiple colors in one mole (brown, red, black) can indicate risk.

6. Diameter Check

Moles larger than 6mm should be checked by a doctor.

7. Bleeding or Itching

Any mole that bleeds, itches, or changes suddenly needs attention.

8. Family History

If skin cancer runs in your family, your risk increases.

9. Use Sunscreen

SPF 30+ sunscreen helps prevent UV damage and skin cancer.

10. Monthly Skin Check

Regular self-examination helps detect abnormalities early.

📚 Skin Disease Awareness

1. Melanoma

Most dangerous type of skin cancer. Early detection is critical.

2. Basal Cell Carcinoma

Common and slow-growing but requires treatment.

3. Actinic Keratosis

Precancerous lesion caused by sun damage.

4. Dermatofibroma

Firm bump, non-cancerous and harmless.

5. Rosacea

Chronic skin condition causing redness and irritation.

6. Psoriasis

Autoimmune disease causing scaly patches.

7. Eczema

Causes itchy, inflamed skin. Not contagious.
